Patch the whole rig in one place.

The bridge between the KYNETYK apps. lynk finds every app running on this machine or your network, shows what each one puts out and lets you patch it wherever it needs to go.

Finds your apps

Spots KYNETYK apps on this machine and across the network, and can launch the ones that aren't open yet.

Drag to patch

Drag from an output to an input to make a route. Or switch to the matrix and see every route on one grid.

One master clock

Pick what drives the tempo, an app, tap or MIDI clock, and swap it mid-show without repatching.

Video the easy way

Spout or Syphon on one machine, NDI across machines. lynk works out which and tells both apps.

A rack for show night

Big BPM and timecode readouts, and a compact always-on-top window that stays out of the way.

Saved as shows

Save the routing as a show file. Open it and the whole rig repatches itself as the apps come online.
