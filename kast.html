Every screen in the building, from one page.

Send video, images and web pages to smart TVs over your network. kast runs on one computer, each TV opens its own address in its browser, and every change shows up on the screens live.

Route in one click

A routing grid of inputs against screens. Click a cell and that content fills the screen. Shift-click to layer it on top.

Place it exactly

Drag content onto a screen, then move, scale and rotate it. It snaps to the centre and edges, or type in exact numbers.

Any way up

Set each screen's ratio and how it's mounted: landscape, portrait either way or upside down.

Live on every screen

Changes land on the TVs as you make them. Videos keep playing, and screens reconnect by themselves if the network drops.

Nothing on the TVs

Any smart TV, tablet or computer with a browser works as a screen. No apps to install, and Identify flashes each one's name so you know which is which.

One picture, many screens

Wall mode spreads one big piece of content across several TVs. Each shows its own slice, and videos stay in sync.
