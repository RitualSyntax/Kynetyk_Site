KYNETYKrakk

Play visuals like an instrument.

Live shader visuals without writing any code. Load a look onto Layer A or B, mix them, and play each with its eight faders. Store scenes and morph between them on the bar.

Eight faders a layer

Every visual gets eight faders. Drag them by hand, map them to MIDI, or hold Kick to push to the max.

Modulation on anything

Stack LFOs, the beat, live audio and random movers on any fader, and watch the live value move.

Scenes that morph

Store looks on scene pads and morph between them, quantised to the bar.

Two layers and master FX

Mix Layer A and B, then run the result through a chain of master effects you can reorder.

Bring your own shaders

Import Shadertoy or Book of Shaders files. rakk finds the controls and suggests faders for you.

Straight to the screen

Send the output full screen to a projector or LED wall, or record it to a video file.
