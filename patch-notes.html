Latest

KYNETYKdekkv0.3.0Update24 Nov 2026

Help guide for beginners

  • New Help tab (Ctrl+4) with a step-by-step beginner's guide: audio setup, the library, a tour of the screen, your first mix, beatgrids, sync, cues and loops, EQ, counting bars, keys, controllers, recording, common problems and a glossary
  • "Show me" buttons in the guide jump to the part of the screen being explained and highlight it
  • "New to DJing?" link on the welcome screen
  • Fixed: pressing CUE while a track was playing paused it but didn't jump back to the cue point
  • Fixed: the headphone preview button in the library stayed lit after Prepare playback stopped
  • Files dekk can't read are now labelled clearly in the library
KYNETYKdekkv0.2.0Update24 Nov 2026

Sync, beatgrids and eject

  • New beat detection that locks onto the kick, so grids line up with the beat
  • Beatgrid editing on every deck with the GRID button: move the grid, nudge or halve/double the BPM, tap tempo, set beat 1, fit the grid to a later kick, or drag the waveform
  • The same grid tools in Prepare, plus a drag grid mode
  • Tempo range setting so tracks like drum & bass come out at 174, not 87
  • Sync now keeps decks locked to the master's beat, and the master hands over when it stops
  • Moving the tempo on any synced deck moves them all
  • Quantise keeps hot cues and jumps on the beat
  • Eject button on each deck
  • Tracks analysed by 0.1 are re-analysed automatically, unless you fixed the grid yourself
KYNETYKkastv0.2.0Major update24 Nov 2026

Wall mode

  • New Wall tab: one big canvas spread across several screens, each showing its own slice
  • Put a screen on the wall with On Wall, then drag, scale and rotate it to match how it hangs
  • Line Up Screens puts wall screens side by side and fits the wall to them
  • Screens snap to each other on the wall
  • Wall column in Routing for one-click content across the whole wall
  • Videos now stay in sync across every screen
