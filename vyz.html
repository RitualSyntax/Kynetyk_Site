KYNETYKvyz

Live visuals that keep time.

A clip grid with layers and groups, effects you shape with envelopes, beat sync and timecode, and outputs cut into slices.

WindowsmacOSLinux

Version 0.1Release notesSource on GitHub

Clip grid

Layers as rows, banks of clips, and columns you fire with one click or a number key. Drag files and generators straight in.

Groups and crossfade

Bundle layers into groups with their own fader and effects, then fade between Group A and Group B.

Effects with envelopes

22 effects plus ISF shaders. Every setting can follow an envelope, triggered by the beat, a bar, a clip or an audio hit.

Beat sync and timecode

Clips can loop to a set number of bars, or go live at a timecode. Tap, MIDI clock or audio sets the tempo.

Outputs in slices

Send the mix, a group or a single layer to any screen, cut into layered slices that snap together.

Control from anywhere

MIDI and OSC learn, Launchpad and APC presets with pad lights, a phone remote, and cues fired from kue.
