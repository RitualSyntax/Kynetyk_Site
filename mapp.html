Surfaces that bend

Quads with true perspective, meshes and smooth meshes. Drag corners or grid points, snap to other surfaces, and nudge with the arrow keys.

Input and output side by side

Pick the part of the source each surface uses on the left, and pin it where it lands on the right.

vyz and rakk, straight in

On Windows, virtual outputs from vyz and rakk come straight from the GPU, with no window to capture. Spout apps work too, and lynk can patch them in.

Masks and blending

Ellipse or drawn masks with feathering, colour controls per surface, and edge blending between overlapping projectors.

Every kind of source

Video, images, generators, ISF shaders, cameras, capture cards and window or screen capture.

Cues and timecode

Save looks as cues with fades. Fire them with GO, timecode from kode, MIDI or OSC.
