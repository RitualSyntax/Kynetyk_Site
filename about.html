ABOUT

Built from the operator's side of the desk.

KYNETYK is an independent, one-person studio making free software for theatre, live events and visual performance. Every app is designed, built and tested by a solo developer who has spent years running video, LED and projection for live shows.

Why KYNETYK exists

Show software tends to fall into two camps. There are the industry standards, which are brilliant but expensive, often tied to one platform and increasingly sold by subscription. Then there are the free tools, which are generous but rarely built for the pressure of a live show.

KYNETYK sits in between. The apps are free and complete, and they are made by someone who has stood behind the desk when a cue doesn't fire, a projector drifts or the playback machine decides to update itself five minutes before doors.

That experience shapes every decision: clear screens you can read from a distance, controls that behave the same way every time, and nothing that gets in the way when it matters.

What KYNETYK stands for

Free, properly

Every app is free with every feature in. No trials, no accounts, no subscriptions, and nothing held back for a paid tier.

Calm on the night

Big, readable controls, clear states and no surprises. The screen should tell you what is about to happen before it happens.

Honest about what it does

If something isn't in yet, the app and the site say so. Patch notes list every change, big or small.

One family

The apps share a clock, pass video straight to each other and are patched together in lynk, so a whole show can run on KYNETYK.

Every platform

Windows, macOS and Linux installers for every release, so the tools go wherever the show goes.

Small, frequent releases

Fixes ship fast. Hotfixes go out the same day where they can, and every release is on the patch notes page.

How it's made

One person, one studio. The apps are written for the desktop so they run offline on show machines, with no account or internet connection needed.

Releases are small and frequent. Every change, whether it's a major update, a bug fix or a hotfix, goes on the patch notes, so you always know what changed before you update a show machine. What's coming next is on the roadmap.

The installers aren't code-signed yet, so your computer may warn you the first time you open one. On Windows click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose Open.

Keeping it going

KYNETYK has no investors, no ads and no paid tier. If the apps help your shows, a donation pays for the time it takes to build them and keeps new tools coming.

Found a bug, or have an idea for a feature? Leave a message on the Ko-fi page. Requests from people using the apps on real shows shape what gets built next.
